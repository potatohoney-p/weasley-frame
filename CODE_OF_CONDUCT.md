# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in Weasley Frame a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and keep
disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained
disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
GitHub contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
